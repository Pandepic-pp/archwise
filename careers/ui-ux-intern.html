UI/UX Design Intern

Design Remote · Internship

Help shape the look and feel of ArchWyse across our marketing site, candidate interview experience, and organization dashboard.

Responsibilities

  • Design new features end-to-end: wireframes, high-fidelity mockups, and interaction details
  • Maintain and extend our design system for consistency across products
  • Collaborate closely with engineers to ensure designs ship as intended
  • Run lightweight user research and iterate based on feedback

Requirements

  • A portfolio showing product design work (school projects welcome)
  • Proficiency in Figma or a similar design tool
  • Strong eye for typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy
  • Ability to explain design decisions clearly

Nice to Have

  • Basic front-end skills (HTML/CSS) to prototype in code
  • Experience designing for B2B SaaS products
