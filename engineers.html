The Philosophy
"AI can already write code. But it can't yet design systems that scale."

That's your competitive edge in every senior interview. But you don't build architectural thinking by reading about it. You build it by defending decisions live, under pressure. That's what ArchWyse simulates.

Technical Depth

Go beyond surface-level answers. Practice defending component choices (when to use a cache, which database fits, how to handle queue backpressure) to an AI that asks why until your reasoning holds up.

Systems Thinking

Articulate the whole system in real time. Practice reasoning through component interactions, scaling bottlenecks, and failure modes, out loud, under interview conditions, with an AI that probes every gap.

Clear Communication

Interviews aren't just technical. Use voice interaction to practice narrating your thought process, handling live clarifying questions, and presenting trade-offs with confidence, before the real thing.

Process

How a session works

Four steps from blank canvas to expert-level feedback in under an hour.

01

Pick a Problem

Choose from 63 real-world interview problems: design Twitter, Netflix, Uber, a URL shortener, and more. Difficulty calibrates to your experience level.

02

Draw & Explain

Use the Excalidraw whiteboard and your voice to draw your architecture and talk through every decision as you would in a real interview.

03

AI Interviews You

ARIA, our Adaptive Real-time Interview Advisor, acts as your interviewer, asking clarifying questions, probing edge cases, and challenging your assumptions in real time.

04

Get Deep Feedback

Receive a scored evaluation across scalability, reliability, trade-offs, and communication quality with specific improvement notes.

About ArchWyse

Built by engineers
who felt the gap.

We spent years preparing for system design interviews at top companies. We read all the books, watched all the videos, could name every component. And we still bombed the first few rounds.

The gap wasn't knowledge; it was practice under pressure. The ability to think through a real problem live, handle follow-up questions, and articulate trade-offs while drawing on a whiteboard.

ArchWyse was built to close that gap, not with more content, but with deliberate practice in a setting that mimics the real thing as closely as possible.

"AI will write most of the code within five years. The engineers who thrive will be those who design the systems AI runs inside."
