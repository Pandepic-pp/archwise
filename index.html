Features

Built to scale your hiring

Every feature is designed around the hiring team's workflow — not just the candidate's experience.

Custom Assessments

Create assessments tailored to your role — your own question, your job description, your seniority level, and a configurable time limit. The AI adapts its expectations to your exact hiring bar.

Async Candidate Experience

Candidates complete the interview on their own schedule — no synchronous slots, no rescheduling. Works globally, reduces drop-off, and removes scheduling overhead entirely.

5-Dimension AI Scoring

Every submission is evaluated on scalability, reliability, trade-offs, technical feasibility, and communication quality — with a clear overall recommendation and written rationale.

Proctoring Built In

Every session is monitored for tab switching, focus loss, and anomalous behavior. You receive a proctoring summary alongside the technical evaluation — no extra setup required.

Team Dashboard

All assessments, candidates, and results in one place. Filter by status, sort by score, and share results with your team — the entire hiring loop in a single view.

Seniority-Aware Rubric

The scoring adapts to the level you're hiring for. A junior engineer is graded differently from a staff engineer — same platform, different expectations, consistent calibration.

ARIA — Adaptive Real-time Interview Advisor

ARIA is ArchWyse's proprietary AI interviewer. It adapts to each candidate in real time — asking contextual follow-up questions, probing gaps in reasoning, and adjusting depth based on what the candidate draws and says. ARIA conducts every assessment with the rigor and consistency that even your best human interviewers can't match at scale.
